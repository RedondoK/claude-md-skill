# Publishing Tomorrow at 10am - Action Plan

## Bad News First

**LinkedIn Articles cannot be scheduled.** 

LinkedIn allows scheduling for regular posts, but NOT for long-form Articles. You must publish manually.

**Why this is better:**
- You'll be there for immediate engagement (critical first 2 hours)
- Can respond to comments in real-time
- Algorithm rewards immediate activity
- More authentic interaction

**Action Plan:**
1. Set alarm for 9:45am tomorrow
2. Have coffee/get settled by 9:50am
3. Open LinkedIn at 9:55am

### Option 2: Draft Now, Publish Tomorrow

**What you CAN do:**
- Create the article in LinkedIn editor NOW
- Save as draft
- Publish tomorrow at 10am

**Steps for Tonight:**

1. **Go to LinkedIn article editor**
   - https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/new/

2. **Paste your entire article**
   - Copy from `linkedin-article.md`
   - Paste section by section
   - Fix any formatting issues

3. **Add cover image** (if using one)
   - Upload `results-dashboard.png`

4. **Preview** to verify formatting

5. **Click "Save as draft"** (top right)
   - NOT "Publish"
   - Look for three dots (...) menu → "Save draft"

6. **Tomorrow at 10am:**
   - Open LinkedIn
   - Go to your drafts: https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/drafts/
   - Click your article
   - Review one final time
   - Click "Publish"
   - Immediately share as post
   - Add GitHub comment
   - Engage for 2 hours

**Advantage:** Less rushed tomorrow, formatting done tonight

**Time tomorrow:** 5 minutes to publish + 2 hours to engage = 2 hours 5 minutes

### Option 3: Use Third-Party Scheduling Tool

**Tools that claim to schedule LinkedIn Articles:**
- Buffer
- Hootsuite
- Later

**Reality:**
- Most only schedule regular POSTS, not Articles
- Those that do Articles often have bugs/limitations
- Requires paid subscription
- First article probably not worth the complexity

**Verdict:** Skip this option for your first article

### Option 4: Publish Tonight, Share Tomorrow

**Alternative approach:**

1. **Publish article tonight** (it goes live)
2. **DON'T share it yet** (it won't appear in feeds)
3. **Tomorrow at 10am, share as a post** with teaser

**Advantage:**
- Article is live and tested
- Sharing is what triggers feed visibility
- Can fix any issues before tomorrow

**Disadvantage:**
- If someone finds it early, loses some "new" factor
- Slightly less authentic "just published" feel

**This actually works pretty well** if you're nervous about tomorrow

## Recommended: Option 2 (Draft Tonight, Publish Tomorrow)

✅ **Article is ready** - No formatting surprises tomorrow

## Common Draft Workflow Questions

**Q: Where do I find my drafts tomorrow?**
A: 
- Method 1: https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/drafts/
- Method 2: Your profile → Articles tab → Drafts
- Method 3: LinkedIn homepage → "Write article" → Your drafts appear at bottom

**Q: Can I edit the draft tomorrow morning?**
A: Yes, but only minor tweaks. Don't rewrite - you already reviewed it tonight.

**Q: What if I forget to publish?**
A: Set TWO alarms. 9:45am and 9:55am. Put phone in different room so you have to get up.

**Q: Can someone see my draft?**
A: No. Drafts are private until you publish.

**Q: Does the timestamp show when I drafted or published?**
A: Published. It will say "Published [time]" when you click Publish tomorrow.
